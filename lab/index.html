01 — The house

Colonial bones, kept honestly

The bungalow went up in 1937 as a British home. The teak joinery, the bay windows, the red-oxide floors that catch the afternoon — none of it was stripped out to make the place look new. What you walk into is the original house, maintained rather than reinvented.

02 — The position

The only address facing the water

Everything in Ooty orbits the lake, but only one hotel stands directly opposite the Boathouse. Step out of the gate and you are already there. The Nilgiri Mountain Railway, the botanical gardens and the market sit within a short drive in every direction.

03 — The weather

Mist arrives before breakfast

At this altitude the cloud sits on the ground until mid-morning. Eucalyptus goes to silhouette, the lake disappears and comes back, and the garden swing stays wet until the sun clears the tree line. It is the reason people keep returning in the same week each year.
