Terms and Conditions

Reservation Confirmation

All reservations made at Lakefront Home Hotel are subject to confirmation upon receipt of a valid booking request and payment.

Check-in/Check-out Times

Check-in time is at 12:00 PM local time, and check-out time is at 11:00 AM local time. Early check-in and late check-out requests are subject to availability and may incur additional charges.

No Pets Policy

Lakefront Home Hotel strictly prohibits pets on the premises. Guests found violating this policy will be subject to additional cleaning fees and may be asked to leave the property without refund.

Damage and Liability

Guests are responsible for any damage caused to the property during their stay. Lakefront Home Hotel reserves the right to charge the credit card on file for any necessary repairs or replacements.

Safety and Security

For the safety and security of all guests, visitors must register at the front desk upon arrival. Unauthorized access to guest rooms or other restricted areas is prohibited.

Compliance with Local Laws

Guests are expected to comply with all local laws and regulations during their stay at Lakefront Home Hotel. Any illegal activity will result in immediate eviction without refund.

Guest Conduct

We strive to provide a peaceful and enjoyable environment for all guests. Disruptive behavior, excessive noise, or any other actions that disturb other guests’ comfort will not be tolerated.

Right to Refuse Service

Lakefront Home Hotel reserves the right to refuse service to anyone for any reason, including but not limited to non-compliance with our policies, inappropriate behavior, or failure to adhere to our terms and conditions. By making a reservation at Lakefront Home Hotel, guests acknowledge and agree to abide by these terms and conditions. Management reserves the right to amend these policies at any time without prior notice.
