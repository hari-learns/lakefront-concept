The house

A British home that never stopped being one

Established in 1937, Lakefront Home Hotel was once a British home called Lourdesville Bungalow. Today this cosy heritage hotel is the only accommodation located directly opposite the iconic Boathouse — a stunning view of the lake, easy access to all the main hotspots, and a top-notch Ooty experience.
