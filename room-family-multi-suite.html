Family Multi-Suite

Set within the original 1937 bungalow, the Family Multi-Suite pairs the heritage woodwork of the house with enough room to actually spread out. It's built around the idea that a family holiday shouldn't mean everyone in one cramped box — there's space to gather together in the evening and room to close a door when someone wants quiet. Wardrobes, seating, and the original teak detailing throughout give it the same character as the rest of the house, just at family scale.
